# Archived: GSI Legal Lift Rules Quick Reference

> **Archive Notice:** This reference was originally published as an information module on `/useful-resources` on Grip Australia.

---

## 1. 2" Two-Hands Pinch (Euro Pinch / Standardised Pinch Blocks)

- **Focus:** Symmetric Bilateral Thumb & Finger Clamping
- **Apparatus:** Two parallel steel plates (or approved 2" solid block) adjusted for hand width, mounted on a vertical 1.9" Olympic loading pin.
- **Ruleset:** Grip Sport International (GSI)
- **Rules:**
  1. **Grip Position:** Overhand pinch grip only. The thumbs must oppose the four fingers across the outer vertical surfaces of the plates.
  2. **Legal Contact:** Hands must be entirely placed on the designated gripping faces. Contact with the center spacers, loading pin, or weights is an immediate foul.
  3. **Clean Pull:** The implement must rise smoothly from the floor without pausing or hitching against the shins or thighs.
  4. **Lockout Criteria:** Lifter stands fully erect with knees locked, hips pushed through, and shoulders back. The implement must remain completely motionless.
  5. **The Down Signal:** Lifter must hold the weight at lockout until the head referee calls "DOWN". The weight must then be guided under grip control down to the floor without dropping.
- **Prohibitions:** No sticky adhesives, resin, or tacky (pure dry magnesium carbonate chalk only). No resting hands or apparatus on the legs at any point.

## 2. Rolling Handle Lift (Rolling Thunder / 60 mm Revolving Deadlift)

- **Focus:** Unilateral Open-Hand Support & Rotational Friction
- **Apparatus:** Standardised 2.375" (60 mm) outer diameter rotating cylindrical handle attached to an Olympic loading pin via a heavy-duty carabiner.
- **Ruleset:** Grip Sport International (GSI)
- **Rules:**
  1. **One-Handed Lift:** Lifted using one hand only (competitor may select left or right hand per attempt).
  2. **Free Rotation:** The handle sleeve must spin freely on its axle. Wedging, cocking, or canting the hand against the side brackets to prevent rotation is strictly illegal.
  3. **Non-Lifting Arm:** The non-lifting hand and arm must remain completely clear of the body, thighs, and lifting apparatus.
  4. **Lockout Criteria:** Hips and knees locked, torso erect, implement held steady off the floor.
  5. **The Down Signal:** Must maintain full grip and control until the referee says "DOWN". Releasing the handle while the load is in the air invalidates the lift.
- **Prohibitions:** No contact between the lifting hand/wrist and the steel side frames. No thumb hooking or strap-like wraps over the top of the fingers.

## 3. The Hub Lift (IronMind Hub / Vintage Plate Hub)

- **Focus:** Pinch Clamping & Fingertip Pad Opposition
- **Apparatus:** Circular raised steel hub (2.875" diameter) fastened to a base plate and suspended from an Olympic loading pin.
- **Ruleset:** Grip Sport International (GSI)
- **Rules:**
  1. **Fingertip-Only Contact:** The weight must be lifted using only the distal pads of the fingers and thumb contacting the circular vertical perimeter of the hub.
  2. **The "Heel Foul":** No part of the palm, palm heel, or web of the hand may contact the flat top surface or the horizontal base plate. The referee must be able to see clear daylight between the palm and the hub surface.
  3. **Vertical Lift:** The implement must be pulled vertically in a clean line without leg assistance or contact.
  4. **Lockout:** Complete standing lockout with hips and knees extended until the audible "DOWN" command.
- **Prohibitions:** No wedging the base plate against clothing or legs. Chalk must be standard dry magnesium carbonate.

## 4. Saxon Bar Deadlift (3" or 4" Rectangular Pinch Bar)

- **Focus:** Two-Handed Broad Flat-Bar Pinching
- **Apparatus:** Hollow structural rectangular steel bar (standardised at 3" x 4" or 3" x 3"), loaded with Olympic plates on rotating or fixed sleeves.
- **Ruleset:** Grip Sport International (GSI)
- **Rules:**
  1. **Double-Overhand Pinch:** Both hands must grasp the flat parallel vertical faces of the bar with thumbs on the rear and fingers on the front.
  2. **No Hooking Underneath:** The tips of the fingers and thumbs must NOT hook under the bottom edge or under-surface of the rectangular tube.
  3. **Lifting Stance:** Competitor may use either a conventional (feet inside hands) or sumo (feet outside hands) stance.
  4. **Lockout Criteria:** The bar must be lifted smoothly to full hip and knee lockout without ramping or resting on the thighs.
  5. **Controlled Return:** After the referee's "DOWN" command, the bar must be guided back to the floor with both hands maintaining contact.
- **Prohibitions:** No under-hooking beneath the bar. No hitching, jerking, or resting the bar on the thighs during the ascent.

## 5. Axle Deadlift (Apollon's Axle 1.9"–2" Non-Rotating Bar)

- **Focus:** Maximal Double-Overhand Support & Thick Bar Grip
- **Apparatus:** A rigid, non-rotating steel bar measuring 1.9" to 2" (48–50 mm) in outer diameter, loaded with standard Olympic plates.
- **Ruleset:** Grip Sport International (GSI)
- **Rules:**
  1. **Double-Overhand Pronated Grip:** Both palms must face toward the lifter (knuckles facing forward/away). Mixed grip (one palm forward, one palm back) is strictly prohibited.
  2. **No Hook Grip:** The thumb cannot be tucked underneath the fingers in an Olympic hook grip. The thumb must wrap over the fingers or remain in a standard open grasp.
  3. **Continuous Ascent:** The bar must travel from floor to lockout in a single continuous movement without downward motion or bouncing.
  4. **Zero Hitching:** Ramping the bar onto the thighs or resting the bar on the knees to regrip is an automatic red light.
  5. **Lockout & Control:** Knees and hips fully locked, shoulders back, holding until the referee's "DOWN" command.
- **Prohibitions:** No straps, wraps, or sticky compounds. No mixed grip. No resting the bar on the thighs.
