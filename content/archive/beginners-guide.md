## Overview

Grip Sport tests hand, wrist, and forearm strength across standardized implements and rule systems. Unlike general gym grip training, competitive grip sport evaluates specialized strength dimensions under strict referee standards.

---

## The Four Core Disciplines

1. **Crushing Grip:** Hand grippers (torsion-spring grippers rated in RGC pounds) testing closed-fist crushing power.
2. **Pinch Grip:** 2-hand Euro Pinch, Flask, Saxon Bar, and block weights (Blobs) testing thumb clamp strength against the fingers.
3. **Thick Bar / Support Grip:** Revolving handles (Rolling Thunder, 2.25" Crusher) and rigid thick axles (Apollon's Axle) testing open-hand support.
4. **Vertical Lift:** Hub lifts and vertical bars where friction and finger pad clamping prevent the tool from slipping.

---

## Starter Equipment Recommendations

- **Torsion-Spring Gripper:** 1 or 2 quality grippers with known RGC ratings.
- **Olympic Loading Pin & Carabiner:** The foundational tool for connecting hubs, rolling handles, and pinch blocks.
- **Block Weight or 2" Pinch Block:** Develops thumb pad and open-hand pinch competence.
- **Gym Chalk:** Plain dry magnesium carbonate (no tacky or liquid adhesives permitted in contest).
