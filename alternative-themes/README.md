# Grip Australia — Alternative Design & Theming Suite

Welcome to the **Grip Australia Alternative Themes** directory. This curated collection combines our highest-rated dark/heritage designs (**Heritage Gold** and **Outback Ochre**), two warm sunlit daylight options (**Sandstone & Coastal Iron** and **Vintage Archival Gazette**), the hardcore industrial foundry theme (**Raw Iron & Mag Chalk**), and our newest addition: **Calibrated Athletic Minimalism** (official Deep Eucalyptus & Wattle Gold federation aesthetic).
