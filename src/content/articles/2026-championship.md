> **2026 Australian Pound-for-Pound Champions**
> * 🏆 **Sarah Rodwell** — Women's Pound-for-Pound Champion (2nd Overall Women)
> * 🏆 **Isaac Pitt** — Men's Pound-for-Pound Champion (2nd Overall Men)
>
