#### Contact Us

Many active Australian grip sport competitors are active on Instagram. If you're struggling to find someone in your area active in grip sport use the Contact Us button and we'll try put you in touch with someone.

#### Join a comp

[Competitions](/competition-calendar) are a great way to meet people, see technique differences, check out different gear and try some grip feats. If you have the equipment and are unable to travel, keep an eye out for online competitions -or consider hosting your own.

#### Gripboard

If there's a question about grip it's probably already been asked on [GripBoard](https://www.gripboard.com/).
