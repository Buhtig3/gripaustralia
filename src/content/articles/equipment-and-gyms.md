#### Supporting gyms with grip equipment

Specialised grip equipment is unlikely to be found at a big chain gym. 

This page hopes to highlight the venues that offer grip equipment to their members and supports the growing grip community in Australia. A minimum requirement to be featured is a lifting pin, a pinch implement and a thick bar or rolling handle. Gyms are encouraged to stock [GSI tracked event](https://www.gripsport.org/lists.php?) handles.

#### ACT

[Ultra Fitness & Strength](https://www.ultrafitnessandstrength.com.au/) - Various Napalm Nightmare handles (2" 2 hand rolling handle, 2x4 pinch blocks), IronMind Little Big Horn, IronMind Hub and other hubs.

![](../../assets/images/equipment-and-gyms-da255c6b.jpg)

#### TAS

[Betapark](https://betapark.com.au/) - Climbing gym with grippers, loading pin, various Grip Genie products including Rolling Grip Thing, Hub, Vertical Bar, Rogue Anvil and various pinch blocks.

  

[Leviathan Strength](https://leviathan-strength-gym.squarespace.com/) - Strongman gym with various grip implements.
